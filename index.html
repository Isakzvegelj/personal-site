About

Discipline on the water.
Curiosity everywhere else.

I grew up in Bled, Slovenia — the son of an Olympic medallist — and dedicated over a decade to the sport of rowing. I represented Slovenia in the men's single sculls at the Paris 2024 Olympics. Official results and profiles are linked below.

Off the water I studied computational cognitive science at the University of Pennsylvania — bridging sport, science, and software. Today I build websites and digital products for businesses in Slovenia and beyond.

Beyond the boat

More than an athlete.
I build things that work.

Rowing taught me pacing, process and pressure. Penn taught me how the mind works — and how technology can amplify it. I've turned that combination into a one-person studio that ships clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites and tools for businesses around Bled and beyond.

I find balance between the demands of rowing and the practical work of building with computers and AI.
