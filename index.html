About

Discipline on the water.
Curiosity everywhere else.

I grew up in Bled, Slovenia — the son of an Olympic medallist — and spent my life chasing excellence on the water. I rowed Slovenia's men's single scull at the Paris 2024 Olympics, made the international finals at World Cups and European Championships, and hold a world record on the indoor rower.

Off the water I studied computational cognitive science at the University of Pennsylvania — bridging sport, science and software. Today I build websites and digital products for clients across Slovenia and beyond, applying the same focus, pacing and precision to code that I do to a race.

Beyond the boat

More than an athlete.
I build things that work.

Rowing taught me pacing, process and pressure. Penn taught me how the mind works — and how technology can amplify it. I've turned that combination into a one-person studio that ships clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites and tools for businesses around Bled and beyond.

Every project gets the same treatment: a clear goal, a sharp plan, and relentless iteration until it's right. Whether you're a hotel, a clinic, an artist, or an idea that needs a home online — I can build it.
